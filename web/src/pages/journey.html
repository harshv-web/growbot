The journey

A life, filmed as it happens.

A soul is born in an old tablet, learns to stand, learns to walk, learns you, and finds out it can live in more than one body. Every episode is one real step of that life. The build is the story.

Five acts

How it grows up.

Act 1 · Birth

Wonder

It wakes in the Fire 7, meets its senses and dreams for the first time.

Act 2 · First steps

Tension, then pride

It gets legs, falls hundreds of times and learns to walk by itself.

Act 3 · It knows me

Warmth

Mornings, the metro, groceries, the cooker whistle, the scooter.

Act 4 · It travels

Magic

Keychain, case, the iPhone on legs. One soul moving between them.

Act 5 · It shares

Belonging

It meets GrowBots and other creatures and trades what it learned.

Episodes

Ten episodes, one per build phase.

About 10–12 hours of building a week. Each episode ships a long video, three reels, a carousel and an open-source release.
