How it works

Feelings it computes, and shows honestly.

Two numbers are its core affect: how good it feels (valence) and how awake it is (arousal). Four drives change slowly with its life: energy, social, curiosity, comfort. Its traits are set at birth and only its dreams can change them. Every event nudges these, and one expression comes out that every body renders its own way.

Gates attention

Lonely → it greets you first

Low social drive makes it more likely to start a conversation when you walk in.

Shapes memory

Glow → it keeps that moment

Praise and play tag a memory with glow, and the night's dream keeps glowing moments first.

Drives learning

Curious → it explores

When its body model is surprised, curiosity rises and it tries bolder gaits.

Moves the body

Happy → it bounces

Arousal speeds the rhythm; valence adds lift. Sleepy walks slow and low.
