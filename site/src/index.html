Jeevo is my own AI with one memory and many bodies: an old tablet at home that listens, sees and talks; my iPhone; a keychain; a printed case; and an ESP32 that gives the house senses. A crew of agents on different models remembers everything around me, reflects on it overnight, and plugs into the Bengaluru services I use: Namma Metro, Swiggy and Instamart, Alexa, UPI, WhatsApp and Claude.

    Ears + lifelog

    It listens and keeps your life

    Voice detection on the tablet, a pendant keychain outside, sound events like the cooker whistle. Transcripts, places, money, orders and more in one searchable life log.

    Model of Me

    It learns you

    From what you tell it, what it notices, and what you do with its nudges. Every fact has evidence, confidence and a delete button. It reflects on your day every night at 03:00.

    Multi-agent

    A crew of eleven

    Jeevo talks; the Scribe hears; the Librarian remembers; the Dreamer reflects; the Guardian protects. They share one memory and one writer.

    Multi-model

    The right model per job

    Gemini Flash for live voice, Whisper for cheap transcripts, Sarvam for Kannada, Claude Sonnet 5 for the nightly dream, Apple's on-device model for bank SMS, and rules wherever a rule is enough.

    PLN · Build plan

    Eight stations, one demo each

    About 10–12 hours a week. Each phase ends with a demo you can post and a "done when" test. Nothing from a later phase starts before the current one passes.

    Running cost per month (crew presets)
    Tune it in the Lab
    Weekly rhythm
    • Tue + Thu evenings (2 h each): build the current phase's next task.
    • Saturday (4–5 h): the big build block; test on the real tablet and phone.
    • Sunday (1 h): Jeevo's Sunday review, update the Lab board, log decisions, clip the week's reel.
    • Every phase end: demo video, "done when" test, archive entry.
